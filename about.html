Who we are

Technology that is simple, reliable and affordable

NextBarisal designs and builds web, mobile and cloud software for growing businesses in Bangladesh. We also set up the everyday technology a business needs: CCTV cameras, office networks, domains and hosting, and reconditioned laptops.

Our office is on Fokir Bari Road in Barishal, so you can always visit us, call us or send a message and talk to a real person.
